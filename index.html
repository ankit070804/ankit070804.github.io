Assistive communication framework

Python, PyTorch, OpenCV, MediaPipe, Transformers

Converts eye blinks into Morse code so people who cannot speak or type can still communicate. It adds Sign Language Grammar (SLG), a Trie for word prediction and a Digital Twin for logging. I fine-tuned and compared Transformer models for SLG-to-English translation and picked Flan-T5-small for the best quality and latency trade-off. It cuts blinks by 26.7% on average, and up to 44.9% for long sentences.
