Assistive communication framework

Python, PyTorch, OpenCV, MediaPipe, Transformers

Converts eye blinks into Morse code so people who cannot speak or type can still communicate. Adds Sign Language Grammar, Trie-based word prediction and a Digital Twin for logging. I fine-tuned and compared Transformer models for SLG-to-English translation and chose Flan-T5-small for the best quality and latency trade-off. It cuts blinks by 26.7% on average, up to 44.9% for long sentences.

UAV localization error prediction

Python, Scikit-learn, SHAP, OpenCV, Ollama (local LLM)

Predicts how far off a drone's visual localization will be, frame by frame, from image, depth, segmentation and pose data (TartanAir). Each prediction is explained with SHAP, and a local LLM turns the top features into a plain-language report. Tested by holding out whole flight sequences, which gives an honest R² of about 0.79 on unseen routes, versus 0.92 on a random split that leaks nearby frames.

Team project built in 6 hours at the myonsite Ascend hackathon.
